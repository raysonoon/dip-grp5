# 📊 NTU Food Discovery Survey — Comprehensive Analysis

**Total Respondents:** 12  
**Target Audience:** Nanyang Technological University (NTU) Students  

---

## Executive Summary
The NTU Food Discovery Survey reveals a strong demand among students for a centralized, real-time campus dining platform. While general satisfaction with food choices is moderate (3.25/5), students face recurring operational friction—specifically long wait times, outdated operational hours, and lack of real-time queue visibility. Interest in a dedicated **NTU Food Guide is very high (4.08/5)**, with moderate-to-high interest in an **AI-powered food recommendation chatbot (3.33/5)**.

## 2. Decision Factors & Discovery Behavior

### Primary Drivers for Food Selection
When deciding where to eat on campus, respondents prioritized factors in the following order:
1. **Price / Affordability**
2. **Location / Proximity to current class**
3. **Taste & Food Quality**
4. **Queue Length & Speed of Service**
5. **Dietary Restrictions** (Halal, Vegetarian)

### Discovery Habits
* **Routine Decisions:** Most students default to existing habits (walking to the nearest spine canteen or hall canteen they already know).
* **Trying Somewhere New:** Half of the respondents rely on trial-and-error (walking around) or recommendations from friends/classmates. Very few rely on existing online NTU maps or social media.

## 3. Key Pain Points & Friction Analysis

| Area | Satisfaction / Difficulty Score | Major Issue Identified |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Overall Satisfaction** | **3.25 / 5** | Moderate; students know where food is, but struggle with logistics. |
| **Finding Short Wait Times** | **66.7% Rate as Difficult** | Long peak-hour queues at North and South Spine without visibility beforehand. |
| **Unpredictable Closures** | **83.3% Experienced Issues** | Going to stalls only to find them closed unexpectedly or out of stock. |
| **Specific Dish Search** | **75.0% Struggle Occasionally/Frequently** | Wanting a specific food item (e.g., Mala, Ban Mian, Halal options) but not knowing where it's served on campus. |

### Top Information Gaps Identified by Students
1. **Live Queue Status / Estimated Wait Times**
2. **Accurate, Up-to-date Opening Hours**
3. **Menu Pricing & Price Updates**
4. **Dietary Certifications (Halal / Vegetarian availability)**
5. **Honest Student Ratings & Reviews**

---

## 4. Feature Demand & Product Roadmap Recommendations

### A. NTU Food Guide Directory (Score: 4.08 / 5)
* **Must-Have Core Features:**
  * Multi-tag filtering (Price $<\$5$, Proximity, Halal, Vegetarian, Air-conditioned vs. Open-air).
  * Up-to-date stall menus with pricing.
  * Operating status indicator (*Open Now*, *Closing Soon*, *Closed*).
  * Campus map integration / directions.

### B. AI Chatbot Assistant (Score: 3.33 / 5)
* **Top User Intent Prompts:**
  1. *"Where can I get cheap food / under $5 near North Spine?"*
  2. *"What food stalls are open right now?"*
  3. *"Recommend something vegetarian/halal near me with short queues."*
  4. *"What's the best chicken rice on campus based on student reviews?"*

---

## 5. Critical Success Factors for Adoption

Based on qualitative feedback from respondents, the success of the platform depends on three pillars:

1. **Data Accuracy:** Information on opening hours and menus *must* be accurate. Outdated data will destroy user trust quickly.
2. **Speed & UI Simplicity:** Students need answers in under 10 seconds while walking between lectures.
3. **Crowdsourced Queue Reporting:** Implementing simple student-led queue status updates or peer reviews to overcome lack of official live queue APIs.
